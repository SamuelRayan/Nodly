Lemon rice

4.9 · Serves 3 · 20 minutes

You need

  • Cooked rice, 2 cups
  • 1 lemon, squeezed
  • Mustard seeds and curry leaves
  • A handful of peanuts
  • Turmeric and salt

Steps

  1. Fry mustard seeds, curry leaves and peanuts in a little oil.
  2. Add turmeric, then the rice and salt. Mix gently.
  3. Turn off the heat and stir in the lemon juice.
