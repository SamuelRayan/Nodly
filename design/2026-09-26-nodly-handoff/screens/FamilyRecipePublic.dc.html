Tomato rasam

Serves 4 · 25 min
Hear Lakshmi tell it
3:12
4.8· 23 ratings 12 cooks sent her a photo

You need

  • Tamarind, a lemon-sized ball
  • 2 tomatoes, chopped
  • 1 spoon rasam powder
  • Turmeric and salt
  • Coriander leaves

Steps

  1. Soak the tamarind in warm water and squeeze out the juice.
  2. Boil the tomatoes with turmeric, salt and the tamarind water.
  3. Add rasam powder, simmer for 5 minutes, top with coriander.
